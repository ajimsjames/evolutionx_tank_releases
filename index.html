Installation Guide

Prerequisite: Unlocked bootloader on Motorola Edge 50 (tank)

1

Reboot to Fastboot Mode

Connect your device to your PC with USB debugging enabled:

adb reboot bootloader
2

Flash Recovery Partitions

Flash boot, vendor_boot, and dtbo images:

fastboot flash boot boot.img
fastboot flash vendor_boot vendor_boot.img
fastboot flash dtbo dtbo.img
fastboot reboot recovery
3

Format Data & Sideload ROM

In Evolution X Recovery: Go to Factory reset > Format data/factory reset. Then select Apply update > Apply from ADB and run:

adb sideload EvolutionX-17.0-20260911-tank-12.2-Unofficial.zip
4

Reboot System

Once sideload completes, select Reboot system now. Welcome to Evolution X 17!

1

Reboot into Recovery

adb reboot recovery
2

Sideload New ZIP (No Data Wipe)

Navigate to Apply update > Apply from ADB and run:

adb sideload EvolutionX-17.0-20260911-tank-12.2-Unofficial.zip
3

Reboot
